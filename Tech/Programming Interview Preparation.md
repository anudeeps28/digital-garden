# Programming Interview Preparation

## Summary
Ujjwal Chadha (10+ years building smart software at Microsoft) shares a practical framework for programming interview prep. The key insight: interviews test your ability to solve problems through discussion, not your ability to regurgitate memorized algorithms.

## Key Ideas
- Build basic computer science fundamentals across four pillars: data structures & algorithms, object-oriented design, databases, operating systems
- Interviews test problem-solving through discussion — not memorization
- Understanding *why* matters more than knowing *what*
- Communication during the interview is as important as the solution

## My Thoughts
The reframe from "memorize algorithms" to "practice problem-solving through discussion" is the most useful insight. It shifts preparation from cramming to thinking out loud.
