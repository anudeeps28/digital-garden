# Steal Like an Artist

## Summary
Austin Kleon's 2012 book, subtitled *10 Things Nobody Told You About Being Creative* and the first in his trilogy. Its premise is that nothing is original: all creative work is built from what came before, so the job is to collect good influences, steal from them honestly, and remix them into something of your own. The rest of the book is practical advice for making that a daily practice.

## Key Ideas
1. **Steal like an artist**: there is good theft (honor, study, credit, transform, remix) and bad theft (copy one source and pass it off as yours). Collect what you love and trace each influence back to its own influences.
2. **Don't wait until you know who you are to get started**: copying your heroes is how you find your own voice, because you can never copy them perfectly, and the gap is where your style is.
3. **Write the book you want to read**: make the thing you wish existed.
4. **Use your hands**: get away from the screen, since physical making brings ideas a screen doesn't.
5. **Side projects and hobbies are important**: the work you do for fun feeds the work you do for real.
6. **Do good work and share it with people**: the idea Kleon expands into [[Show Your Work]].
7. **Geography is no longer our master**: you can find your community online, wherever you live.
8. **Be nice**: the world is a small town, so give credit and be generous.
9. **Be boring**: steady routines, finances and health are what leave energy for the work.
10. **Creativity is subtraction**: constraints and choosing what to leave out are what make the work.
