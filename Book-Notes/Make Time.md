# Make Time

## Summary
Jake Knapp and John Zeratsky, two former Google designers who created the Design Sprint, wrote this 2018 book about taking back your attention from two default forces: the **Busy Bandwagon** (the culture of filling every hour with meetings and email) and **Infinity Pools** (apps and feeds with endless content). It is not a productivity system for doing more. It is a daily loop for spending time on what you actually care about, with a menu of tactics you try out and keep only if they work for you.

## Key Ideas
- **Highlight**: choose one thing each day that deserves your best time and protect that time. This is [[The Daily Highlight]].
- **Laser**: defend focus by making distractions harder to reach, for example a distraction-free phone, logging out, and scheduling email instead of reacting to it.
- **Energize**: focus depends on energy, so move, eat real food, sleep, and take breaks without screens.
- **Reflect**: a short note each night on what worked, then adjust tomorrow.
- **Tactics, not rules**: the book offers dozens of tactics and expects you to experiment with them, not adopt all of them.
