# Keep Going

## Summary
Austin Kleon's 2019 book, subtitled *10 Ways to Stay Creative in Good Times and Bad*, which closes his trilogy. *Steal Like an Artist* is about starting and *Show Your Work!* is about sharing, and this one is about lasting. It covers how to keep making things for a lifetime despite distraction, self-doubt, burnout and success. Kleon's answer is a daily practice that matters more than any single outcome.

## Key Ideas
1. **Every day is Groundhog Day**: treat each day as the only one you have to work with. Routines and lists keep you moving when motivation won't.
2. **Build a bliss station**: protect a time and a place each day where you're unreachable.
3. **Forget the noun, do the verb**: chase the activity (writing, painting) rather than the title (writer, painter) or its rewards.
4. **Make gifts**: make some work for people you love rather than for the market, so the market doesn't take over everything.
5. **The ordinary + extra attention = the extraordinary**: you don't need an exciting life to make interesting work, only careful attention to the life you have.
6. **Slay the art monsters**: being a genius doesn't excuse being cruel. Make work that helps.
7. **You are allowed to change your mind**: uncertainty and changing direction are part of the work.
8. **When in doubt, tidy up**: tidying and playing around unblock you.
9. **Demons hate fresh air**: take a walk every day.
10. **Plant your garden**: creative work has seasons, and patience is part of it.
