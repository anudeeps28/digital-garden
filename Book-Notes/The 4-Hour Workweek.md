# The 4-Hour Workweek

## Summary
Tim Ferriss' 2007 book makes the case for leaving the "work until retirement, then live" script. Ferriss describes the **New Rich**: people who value time and mobility over accumulated money and design their lives around them now. The method is a four-step process, **DEAL**, for freeing up your time and your location.

## Key Ideas
- **Definition**: replace vague goals with concrete "dreamlines". Price out the life you actually want, which usually costs far less than you assume.
- **Elimination**: cut most of the work. Apply the [[Pareto Principle]] to find the 20% that matters, use Parkinson's Law (work expands to fill the time available) by setting short deadlines, and keep a low-information diet.
- **Automation**: hand off or systematize what's left, using virtual assistants and a small automated "muse" business that produces income without constant attention.
- **Liberation**: negotiate remote work and take mini-retirements spread through life instead of saving it all for the end.
- The deeper argument is against [[The Deferred Life Plan]].
