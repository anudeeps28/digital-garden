# You Are Your Training Data

## Idea
Just as an AI model's personality is entirely shaped by its training data, your identity is shaped by the inputs you consume — habits, people, environment, media.

## Definition
Before training, an AI model is a blank notebook — no personality, no opinions. The training data *is* the notebook. Change the pages, and you don't get a worse notebook — you get a different one. The same applies to people: there is no protected "real you" underneath waiting to emerge regardless of inputs. You are the books you finished, the people you spend time with, the shows you rewatch, the city you grew up in. Change enough inputs for long enough, and you don't become a worse version of yourself — you become someone else entirely. The insight: stop blaming circumstances and start curating your inputs like they're the only thing building you — because they are.

## Source
Anudeep Sharma's "Models & Mindsets" series (#4, July 2026). The metaphor draws on the machine learning concept of [[Supervised Learning]] training pipelines, while the life philosophy echoes Jean Anthelme Brillat-Savarin's 1826 "Tell me what you eat and I will tell you what you are" and Jim Rohn's "You are the average of the five people you spend the most time with."

**Paths** — Understanding this leads directly to [[Your Environment Is Your Destiny]] and [[Environment Design]] — once you accept that inputs shape outputs, the next step is deliberately designing those inputs.

**Neighbors** — This lives near [[Consistency Over Intensity]] and [[Consistency]], because changing your training data isn't a one-time event but a sustained shift in daily habits that compounds over time.

**Clash** — This pushes against the comforting belief in an innate, fixed "true self" — and it can be taken too far into determinism, ignoring the role of agency and deliberate choice that [[The Dichotomy of Control]] emphasizes.
