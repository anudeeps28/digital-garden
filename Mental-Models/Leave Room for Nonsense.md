# Leave Room for Nonsense

## Idea
Intelligence without playfulness is the fastest way to become boring, and the trap is reached one reasonable decision at a time.

## Definition
Ask when you last did something pointless, with no output and nothing to show for it. For many optimisers the answer is unsettling: the books are useful, the rest is scheduled, the walk has a podcast. None of those choices was bad, and every one of them worked, which is why the drift went unnoticed. Somewhere along the way "no point" got filed as "waste," and a life got built with no room for nonsense. The smartest people say ridiculous things to make someone laugh and get excited about small, meaningless things. Silliness is not the opposite of intelligence; it is one of its traits.

## Source
Personal reflection, from a widely shared reel whose original author is unattributed. The sentiment has older forms: the proverb "all work and no play makes Jack a dull boy" (17th century) and the line "creativity is intelligence having fun," commonly misattributed to Einstein.

## Compass

**Roots** — *where this comes from*
It is the personal-life face of [[Toxic Productivity]]: when every hour must earn its keep, play is the first thing to be optimised away.

**Paths** — *where this leads*
Restoring it means deliberately scheduling the unscheduled, which is what [[JOMO (Joy of Missing Out)]] and [[Slow Productivity]] make space for.

**Neighbors** — *what lives nearby*
[[You Cannot Compete with Someone Who Is Having Fun]] makes the same claim from the outside: play is not a leak of seriousness but its fuel.

**Clash** — *what pushes against this*
[[The Daily Highlight]] and similar systems assume time should be directed on purpose; the tension is that nonsense stops being nonsense the moment it becomes a scheduled block.
