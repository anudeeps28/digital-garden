# Delay Is Capacity Catching Up

## Idea
Some journeys take longer because the version of you who can arrive does not exist yet, so the delay is not empty time but the building of that person.

## Definition
Odysseus spends ten years getting home and the man who reaches Ithaca is not the man who left. Most of us assume that if something is meant for us it should be a straight line, and when it takes longer we conclude we missed our shot. But some destinations require you to be built on the way. Learning to code took years, and the syntax was never the point; those years produced someone who can sit inside a hard problem without flinching. Delay is capacity catching up to desire. Sometimes you really are late. But often the person who can live inside the arrival is still becoming.

## Source
Personal reflection after watching Christopher Nolan's *The Odyssey* (July 2026), an adaptation of Homer's epic composed roughly 2,700 years ago.
