# Bliss Station

## Idea
Protect a time and a place each day where you can't be reached, so there's always room for the work that matters to you.

## Definition
A bliss station is a regular slot, a physical spot, or ideally both, where the outside world can't get in: no news, no messages, no obligations to anyone. It might be the hour before everyone wakes up, a particular chair, or a walk without your phone. Its value isn't productivity in the moment. It's having a reliable space where your own ideas can come up without other people's demands crowding them out. Kleon admits most people can't have both a perfect room and a perfect hour, but even a small, consistent one counts. The space works because it's predictable: protecting it daily turns showing up into a habit instead of a decision.

## Source
Austin Kleon, *[[Keep Going]]* (2019), principle #2. Kleon takes the term from mythologist Joseph Campbell in *The Power of Myth* (1988, with Bill Moyers), who described needing a room or an hour each day where "you don't know what was in the newspapers that morning" and you can "experience and bring forth what you are and what you might be".

## Compass

**Roots** — *where this comes from*
It is [[Environment Design]] applied to attention: rather than relying on willpower to ignore distractions, build a place and time where they can't reach you.

**Paths** — *where this leads*
A bliss station is where [[Deep Work]] actually happens, and the obvious home for [[The Daily Highlight]]. Kept up daily, it becomes [[Consistency]] with almost no friction.

**Neighbors** — *what lives nearby*
[[JOMO (Joy of Missing Out)]] is the attitude that makes being unreachable feel good rather than anxious. [[Your Environment Is Your Destiny]] makes the larger version of the claim: the places you spend time shape what you become.

**Clash** — *what pushes against this*
Not everyone can buy a quiet hour. Caregivers, shift workers and people in crowded homes may never have a door to close, and advice to "protect your time" can ignore who pays for that protection. In those cases the station might be a ten-minute walk, and that still counts.
