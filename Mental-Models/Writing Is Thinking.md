# Writing Is Thinking

## Idea
Writing is not transcribing thoughts you already have; it is the process by which you discover what you think, so outsourcing it outsources the thinking.

## Definition
You sit down not knowing what you mean, and by the end of the paragraph you do. That transformation is the entire value of writing, and the finished text is a by-product. When an AI produces the text, you receive the by-product and skip the transformation, which means you end the session knowing exactly what you knew at the start, only now with a document that suggests otherwise. Because everyone can now produce fluent text, the person who still does the thinking is the one who stands out.

## Source
Personal reflection, sharpened by Leslie Lamport's line "If you're thinking without writing, you only think you're thinking" and by Paul Graham's essay "Writes and Write-Nots" (2024), which predicts a split between people who can still write, and therefore think, and those who cannot.
