# Elon First Trillionaire

## Summary
A content piece using Elon Musk becoming the first trillionaire as a hook to discuss resilience and persistence. The real lesson isn't about the money (most is illiquid stock) — it's that he's failed in public more times than most people have tried anything once.

## Key Ideas
- The trillion-dollar valuation is tied up in stock — not the interesting part
- The interesting part: making electric cars work when experts said it couldn't be done, building self-landing rockets, rescuing stranded astronauts
- The mindset matters more than the outcomes
- "The winner has failed more times than the loser has ever tried"
- Instead of criticizing builders, learn from them

## Script Draft

**Hook:** "The world just got its first trillionaire — and almost everyone's looking at the completely wrong thing."

**Body:** Yeah, his money's all tied up in stock. He can't really cash it out. Cool. That's not the interesting part. This is the guy who made electric cars happen when every expert said it couldn't be done. Who built rockets that land themselves. There's a whole book on how he thinks — and the takeaway? It's not luck. It's not genius. It's the mindset.

**Close:** That guy has failed in public more times than most of us have tried anything once. Rockets blew up. People wrote him off again and again. He just kept going. The winner has failed more times than the loser has ever tried. So next time you catch yourself piling on someone who's actually building something — maybe ask what you could learn from them instead.
