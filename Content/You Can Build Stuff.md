# You Can Build Stuff

## Summary
A content piece arguing that AI hasn't made effort pointless — it's the exact opposite. If you can build anything now (video, images, code, writing), then the only thing between you and what you want is whether you do the work. The barrier is gone; the excuse is gone; all that's left is effort.

## Key Ideas
- AI enables non-specialists to solve problems outside their domain (an engineer building marketing pages)
- The narrative "AI does everything so why try" has it backwards — AI means anyone can try
- The limiting factor has shifted from knowledge/skill to effort/execution
- "This is the best time in history to be alive. Go act like it."
