# How to Make More Money in Your Job

## Summary
Ali Abdaal interviews Jenny Wood, a former Google executive who founded Google's largest career development program, about nine practical strategies for earning more money as an employee. The core argument: 93% of people are employees, not entrepreneurs, and there's a clear playbook for advancing within a company that doesn't get enough attention.

## Key Ideas
- Become your boss's favorite employee — not by sucking up, but by making their life easier and aligning with their goals
- Your boss relationship affects your entire well-being, not just your career — it can be more impactful than your relationship with your partner
- Visibility matters — it's not enough to do good work, people need to see the work and its impact
- Communicate with conviction — stop rambling, lead with recommendations, frame ideas in terms of value to others
- Ask powerful questions in career development conversations — be specific about what you want
- Build relationships across the organization, not just with your direct manager
- Have dedicated 30-45 min career conversations — separate from regular check-ins

## Quotes / Excerpts
> "Most people anchor on what's in it for me. Flip it to what's in it for them."

> "Your boss can determine the future of your success, your career, and your financial freedom."

## My Thoughts
The "become your boss's favorite" framing is uncomfortable but honest — it's really about managing up effectively. The insight about framing everything in terms of value to others (not self) is the most actionable takeaway.
