# 80,000 Hours Career Guide

## Summary
80,000 Hours (named after the approximate number of hours in a career) provides evidence-based guidance for making career decisions that maximize both personal fulfillment and positive social impact. Their core argument: you don't have to choose between a satisfying career and having a meaningful impact.

## Key Ideas
- Focus on the world's most pressing challenges — problem selection matters more than most career advice acknowledges
- Explore strategically — avoid locking in too early, build career capital that keeps options open
- Leverage your comparative advantage rather than following generic advice
- Priority problem areas: AI safety, biosecurity, nuclear risk, factory farming, global health, climate change
- Career paths aren't just "earn to give" — AI policy, founding organizations, journalism, and operations can have outsized impact

## Quotes / Excerpts
> "You don't have to choose between a satisfying career and having a positive social impact."

## My Thoughts
The framework inverts conventional career advice by starting with "which problems matter most?" rather than "what am I good at?" — then finding the intersection. The emphasis on career capital and optionality resonates with the startup idea of keeping your burn rate low until you find product-market fit.
