# Dead Internet Theory

## Idea
Most of what moves through the internet was never a person — and as AI agents replace human browsing, the web is splitting into a surface for humans and an infrastructure for machines.

## Definition
In 2021, a user on Agora Road's Macintosh Cafe forum posted "Dead Internet Theory" — the claim that most internet content and traffic is generated by bots, not humans. Parts were paranoid (celebrities as CGI), but the shape was right, and nobody had to make it true — it just happened. The deeper implication: the internet was designed for humans — pretty pages, emotional headlines, clickable ads. An AI agent doesn't need any of that. It doesn't get charmed, doesn't click ads, just goes for what's useful. So products split into two layers: one surface built for human enjoyment, one built for machine parsing. Being a person online used to be the default. It's becoming something you choose on purpose.

## Source
"IlluminatiPirate" on Agora Road's Macintosh Cafe forum (2021), though the concept circulated on 4chan in the late 2010s. Anudeep Sharma's content (Instagram, August 2026) extends the theory to the AI agent era — the split between human-facing and agent-facing internet.
