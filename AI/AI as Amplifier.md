# AI as Amplifier

## Idea
AI doesn't make everyone smarter or dumber — it amplifies whatever you already are.

## Definition
If you use AI to avoid thinking — to outsource the hard work of figuring things out — you lose the cognitive muscle. Your brain checks out. MIT researchers found that ChatGPT users showed weaker neural connectivity and couldn't even recall content from essays they'd just written. But if you use AI to *support* your thinking — as a thought partner, a tool to test ideas, a source of information you evaluate — you get sharper and faster. Three modes: **Autopilot** ("do it for me") makes you dumber. **Co-pilot** ("do it with me") keeps you sharp. **Coach** ("teach me, quiz me, make me reason") makes you smarter. Most people are stuck in autopilot. The winners use coach mode.

## Source
Anudeep Sharma's content (YouTube, August 2026), drawing on MIT's "Brain on LLM" study (preliminary, not yet peer-reviewed) and Stanford's critical thinking framework. The three-mode framework (autopilot/co-pilot/coach) is the note's distinctive contribution.
