# AGI Arrives in Phases, Not on a Day

## Idea
There will be no single model release we can point to as the day AGI arrived; general capability comes in levels, unevenly, and is mostly noticed after the fact.

## Definition
Every big model release brings two loud camps: "AGI is here, we don't need to work" and "this is all hype, it can't do anything." Both treat AGI as a switch, either flipped or not. It is closer to a gradient with several dimensions. A model can be superhuman at one task, merely competent at another, and unable to finish a third without a human stepping in, all at once. Agents show this today. They are easy to build and get real work done, but almost none of them can carry a messy task all the way to the end without asking for input. The useful question is not "is it AGI yet?" but "at which tasks, at what level, and with how much autonomy?"

## Source
Captured from my own reel on overhyped AI buzzwords (October 2026).

The closest formal version is Google DeepMind's *Levels of AGI: Operationalizing Progress on the Path to AGI* (Morris et al., November 2023). It scores systems on two axes, performance and generality. The performance levels are Emerging, Competent (50th percentile of skilled adults), Expert (90th), Virtuoso (99th) and Superhuman, and autonomy is tracked as a separate dimension. The paper argues for measuring a path rather than waiting for one endpoint, and rated the chatbots of the time as "Emerging AGI".

## Compass

**Roots** — *where this comes from*
This applies [[Linear Minds in a Nonlinear World]] to AI discourse: we expect one clean threshold, but capability grows unevenly and across many dimensions. It is also why [[Judging AI on a Version That Is Gone]] misleads, since each snapshot sits at a different level for different tasks.

**Paths** — *where this leads*
If AGI comes task by task, the practical response is [[A Job Is a Bundle of Tasks]]: watch which tasks in your bundle cross the line, not the headlines. For agents, the gap between "does the work" and "finishes the work" is what a [[Needs-You Inbox]] is designed around.

**Neighbors** — *what lives nearby*
It sits beside [[AI Is a Tool, Not Magic]], which deflates the same hype from the side of expectations, and [[Consensus Is Not Evidence]], since "everyone says AGI is here" tells you about the crowd, not the model.

**Clash** — *what pushes against this*
"It arrives in phases" can turn into a way of never admitting a threshold has been crossed. Some capability jumps really are discontinuous, and a gradualist frame can make people complacent about them.
