# English Is the Programming Language

## Idea
Agentic coding tools take instructions in plain sentences, so the word "code" in their names gatekeeps people who could already use them.

## Definition
You describe what you want the way you would explain it to a friend, and the tool goes and does it. Engineers have a head start because knowing how systems fit together helps, but it is not a requirement for most of what people actually want to build. The knowledge arrives on the way: you learn what a file does because you needed it to do something, and what broke because it broke on you. The self-taught path was always there; it was just slow. Now it is faster and cheaper, and nobody is unqualified for it, only early.

## Source
Personal reflection on how people react to Claude Code. The framing traces to Andrej Karpathy's January 2023 post "The hottest new programming language is English" and his February 2025 coinage of "vibe coding," which Collins named Word of the Year for 2025.
