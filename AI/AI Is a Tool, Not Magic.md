# AI Is a Tool, Not Magic

## Idea
When you don't know how something works, it feels like magic — and when something feels like magic, you wait instead of act.

## Definition
The AI hype cycle exploits a simple gap: most people can name every model that dropped last year but can't explain how a single one works under the hood. The "magic box" reads enormous amounts of text, learns what tends to follow what, and predicts the next word — that's next-token prediction. It's a system with rules, and you can learn the rules. Once you understand what factors govern the output (temperature, context window, training data quality, prompt structure), you stop waiting for a better model and start getting better results with the model you have. AI fluency is not about using more expensive tools — it's about understanding the tool you're already using.

## Source
Anudeep Sharma's "Models & Mindsets" series (#5, August 2026). The demystification of AI draws on foundational concepts in [[LLM (Large Language Model)]] and [[Tokens]], and the broader principle of [[Prompt Engineering]] as a learnable skill rather than an arcane art.
